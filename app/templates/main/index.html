How it works

Three steps from application to repayment

1

Apply

Choose an amount and a tenure. Your EMI updates as you type, and you upload one supporting document.

2

Get approved

A loan officer reviews your application, leaves remarks, and disburses the money once it is approved.

3

Repay

Pay your installments one by one from your dashboard. The loan closes automatically after the last one.
